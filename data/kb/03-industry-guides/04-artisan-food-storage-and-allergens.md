## What's in our food category

Single-origin chocolate, specialty coffee and tea, olive oil, vinegar, honey, preserves, cured meats (where regulation permits), small-batch cheese, confectionery, baked goods (limited), spices, and a curated wine and spirits selection (markets where lawful).

We do not list mass-produced food rebadged as artisan, dropshipped imported ingredients, or supplements that are food-shaped (those go through Beauty & Wellness).

## Storage at home

| Category | Storage | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Chocolate | 16–18°C, 50–60% RH, dark | Refrigerator only as last resort, in airtight container, allow to temper before opening |
| Olive oil | Cool, dark | Once opened, use within 90 days for best flavor; oxygen is the enemy |
| Coffee (whole bean) | Airtight, room temp, away from light | Refrigerator/freezer is debatable; we do not recommend |
| Tea | Airtight, away from light, away from kitchen smells | Tea absorbs odors aggressively |
| Honey | Pantry, never refrigerator | Crystallization is reversible (warm-water bath, never microwave) |
| Cured meats | Refrigerated, original wrapping | Allow to come to room temperature before serving |
| Cheese | Refrigerated, in cheese paper or wax paper, never plastic wrap | Plastic suffocates the cheese |
| Wine (still) | 12–14°C, on its side | If unable, drink within 2–3 months of receipt |
| Spirits | Upright, room temp | Wine rules do not apply to high-proof spirits |
| Preserves & vinegar | Pantry until opened, refrigerator after | Many pickled items are shelf-stable both before and after opening — check label |

## Use-by, best-by, and safety

We distinguish strictly between *use-by* (safety) and *best-by* (quality).

**Use-by** is a hard limit. We will not ship anything within 14 days of a use-by date. If a use-by date is missed during transit (rare but possible), contact concierge — we replace immediately.

**Best-by** is a quality indication. Most chocolate, oils, honey, vinegars, and dry goods remain perfectly enjoyable past the best-by, with some flavor drift.

We do not relabel or extend dates on any product.

## Allergens

Every product page declares allergens with both the EU "Annex II" 14-category framework and the FDA Big-9 framework for US listings. We declare even at trace level (i.e., "made in a facility that handles…").

The 14 EU declared allergens are: cereals containing gluten, crustaceans, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, milk (including lactose), tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites (>10 mg/kg), lupin, molluscs.

The FDA Big-9 are: milk, eggs, fish, crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soy, sesame.

## Allergen mistakes

If you receive a product whose allergen profile differs from what was declared, this is a serious incident. We treat it as such. Contact concierge immediately — we (a) refund or replace, (b) flag the brand internally for investigation, and (c) review every other order shipped under that batch.

## Provenance

For specialty items, the product page declares: country of origin, region (where applicable), producer name, harvest year (for olive oil, wine, certain teas), variety / cultivar, and any third-party certification (DOP, IGP, Single Estate, Fair Trade, organic).

We have rejected provenance claims from approximately 1 in 7 brands during onboarding. The top mismatch is "single origin" claims that are actually blends, and harvest-year claims that pre-date the actual bottling year.

## Customs and food shipping

Food crosses borders less easily than non-food. We ship our food category to a subset of about 18 markets. We do not ship:

- Fresh dairy or eggs internationally.
- Fresh meat internationally.
- Honey to certain Australian states (biosecurity).
- Soil-borne products (some preserves) to New Zealand.
- Spices to India and a small set of other markets where private import is restricted.

Refrigerated food ships in passive cold packaging (insulated liner + frozen gel) on next-day or 2-day service only. We do not ship refrigerated food on standard.

## Wine and spirits

Where lawful, we ship wine and spirits on adult-signature delivery. Markets with restrictions: many US states (we comply with state-by-state direct-shipping rules), Saudi Arabia, UAE (alcohol), Indonesia, Malaysia, Brunei. We do not currently ship wine to mainland China.

## Tasting notes

For wine, coffee, tea, chocolate, and olive oil, every product page includes a tasting note written by our editorial team after a blind panel. We do not republish maker tasting notes verbatim. The panel meets weekly at our New York studio.
