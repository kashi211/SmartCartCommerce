## What we list, and what we don't

Our beauty curation is the strictest of any of our verticals, because skin and body products carry the highest health stakes. We accept brands across skincare, color cosmetics, fragrance, body care, hair care, and wellness supplements. We do not list anti-aging products that make medical claims (we do not list "wrinkle-eliminating," "stretch-mark erasing," or any product whose marketing implies a clinical effect). We do not list products that include compounds on our restricted list (below).

## Our restricted ingredient list

SmartCartCommerce products do not contain any of the following:

- Microbeads (already banned in most markets, but we exclude pre-existing inventory too).
- Methylparaben, propylparaben, butylparaben, ethylparaben (paraben class as a whole).
- Triclosan, triclocarban.
- Hydroquinone above 2%, and we do not list products marketing whitening / brightening as their primary function.
- Coal tar dyes (FD&C and D&C colorants beyond an allowed list).
- Phthalates (DBP, DEHP, DEP) — phthalates appear in many fragrances; our perfume brands have signed declarations confirming exclusion.
- BHA / BHT as preservatives (the synthetic preservative class — distinct from the salicylic acid family also called BHA in cosmetics nomenclature).
- Formaldehyde and formaldehyde-releasing agents (DMDM hydantoin, quaternium-15, imidazolidinyl urea, diazolidinyl urea, sodium hydroxymethylglycinate).
- PFAS (per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances) of any class.
- Talc not certified asbestos-free.
- Ingredients tested on animals after 2013 (the EU ban year, which we use as a global standard regardless of where the product ships).

This list is reviewed quarterly. We add to it. We have removed three brands from the platform in the last year because of an ingredient change post-listing.

## Allergen disclosure

Every product page lists the full INCI ingredient deck. We highlight the EU 26 fragrance allergens where present. We highlight common contact-allergen actives (lanolin, propylene glycol, MI, formaldehyde donors as a category) at the top of the deck, not buried.

## Patch testing

We recommend a 24-hour patch test for any new product, especially leave-on formulations. The product page has a one-paragraph "How to patch test" instruction.

## Storage

**Most skincare** is stable at room temperature in a dark, dry place. Bathroom storage is fine for most products but suboptimal for active ingredients (vitamin C in particular, which oxidizes faster with heat and humidity).

**Refrigerated skincare** — some of our brands recommend refrigeration for stability or sensorial reasons. We follow the maker's guidance; the product page says.

**Sun-sensitive actives** — retinoids, AHAs, vitamin C — should be stored opaquely. Most are packaged that way.

**Fragrance** — store in original box, away from light. Heat and UV degrade citrus and floral top notes within months.

## Shelf life and PAO

**PAO (Period After Opening)** is the small jar symbol on the back of the product, with a number ("12M," "24M") indicating the recommended use period after opening. This is more relevant than the manufactured-on date for most products.

We do not list expired stock. We rotate inventory aggressively. If you ever receive a product within 60 days of its sell-by, contact concierge for a free replacement of the same item.

## Reactions

If you experience a reaction — irritation, breakout, redness — stop using the product, document it (a photo helps), and contact concierge. We process reaction-based returns of opened beauty products as standard. We pass anonymized reaction reports to the brand quarterly so they can iterate.

For severe reactions, see a healthcare professional first; talk to us second.

## Pregnancy and medical conditions

Some actives are not recommended during pregnancy (high-strength retinoids, some essential oils). Where applicable, the product page flags this. We are not a medical resource; talk to your prenatal provider.

## Disposal

We accept beauty packaging back via our take-back program. Glass bottles are washed and reused or down-cycled. Pumps are separated. Mixed-material packaging is recycled where streams exist. Empty refillable formats stay with their refill program.
