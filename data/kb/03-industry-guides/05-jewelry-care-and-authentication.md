## Authentication

Every fine jewelry and watch piece on SmartCartCommerce is authenticated by our in-house gemology team before listing. Authentication includes:

- Hallmark verification for precious metal.
- Independent grading reports for diamonds above 0.30 ct (GIA preferred; AGS or HRD accepted from established maker partners).
- Color stone disclosure (treatment status: heat, oil, irradiation — we disclose all).
- Origin disclosure where verifiable (Burma, Mozambique, Colombia, etc.).
- For watches: serial number cross-check against the manufacturer registry; movement opening to confirm caliber on pieces above $5,000 USD; full service history when from a documented owner.

Each piece ships with a certificate from our gemology team plus the maker's documentation. The certificate is a real piece of paper — not just a PDF — printed on cotton stock with embossed authentication.

For pre-owned pieces (a small but growing part of our business), we additionally verify provenance through our consignment-source vetting process.

## Care — daily

**Take it off when you sleep, exercise, swim, or work with chemicals.** This is the single most useful jewelry-care habit. Pearls and opals especially do not survive shower steam well over time.

**Put it on last when dressing, take it off first when undressing.** Avoids snags, hairspray exposure, and lotion contact.

**Store separately.** Diamond will scratch nearly anything else in the box, including other diamonds. Soft-lined compartments or individual pouches.

## Care — material specific

**Gold (yellow, rose, white).** Wipe with a soft cloth after wear. Periodic warm-water soap soak with a soft-bristle brush. White gold is rhodium-plated; the plating wears thin over years (our brands typically replate at 18-month intervals on demand; included in $200 Circle repair credit).

**Platinum.** Develops a soft patina. Polish to high shine if you prefer; live with the patina if you prefer. Equally correct.

**Silver.** Tarnishes. Polish with a non-abrasive silver cloth. Wear it. Silver in a drawer tarnishes faster than silver on a wrist.

**Diamond.** Cleans easily — soap, water, soft toothbrush. Avoid ultrasonic on filled or treated stones.

**Emerald.** Almost always oiled. Never ultrasonic. Never steam. Soft cloth, occasional soap-water rinse.

**Ruby and sapphire.** Hardest of color stones. Can take ultrasonic if the setting allows. Avoid heat changes on heat-treated stones.

**Opal, turquoise, pearl, coral, amber.** Soft and porous. Soft cloth only. No ultrasonic, no steam, no household cleaners, no perfume contact.

**Pearl** specifically — wipe with a damp soft cloth after wear. Restring annually for frequently-worn strands.

## Watches

**Service intervals.** Mechanical movements should be serviced every 5–7 years for accuracy; quartz typically every 3–4 years for battery + seal. Service is included in purchase pricing for the first interval on watches above $5,000 USD.

**Water resistance.** Stated rating in meters/ATM is for static pressure, not active use. A 30m / 3 ATM watch survives splashes, not swimming. A 100m / 10 ATM watch is suitable for swimming. For diving, look for ISO 6425 certification.

**Crown and pushers.** Always screw the crown back in (where applicable) before exposing to water. Never operate pushers underwater unless explicitly rated for it.

**Magnetism.** Modern phones, fridges, laptops with magnets — they affect mechanical accuracy. Demagnetization is a 5-minute service we offer free at any showroom.

## Resizing & repair

Most rings can be resized within +/- 2 sizes without structural concern, depending on setting. Eternity bands cannot be resized; we offer remake or alteration paths instead. Ring resizing is free on first sizing within 60 days of purchase (most fine jewelry brands on the platform participate).

Pearls are restrung at our New York and Los Angeles ateliers. Lead time 3–5 weeks.

## Insurance

We provide a complimentary appraisal document with every fine jewelry purchase above $1,500 USD. Most home-insurance riders accept this document for scheduled-jewelry coverage. Beyond a certain value (typically $25,000+), we recommend a separate policy.

## Trade-up program

A small number of partner brands accept their own pieces back at original retail value as credit toward a new purchase from the same brand. We administer this on the customer's behalf.

## Take-back

Gold, silver, platinum, and palladium pieces are eligible for our take-back program at end of life (or end of love). We melt and re-refine; documented chain-of-custody. Stones are recovered and reused or returned to the customer.
