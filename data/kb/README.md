# SmartCartCommerce — Internal Knowledge Base

The canonical home for SmartCartCommerce's company documentation. Customer-facing policies, brand-side operations, customer-support playbooks, product feature documentation, editorial standards, and selected internal Slack archives.

This repository is the source of truth. If a public-facing page on smartcartcommerce.com or in our app contradicts something here, this document wins, and someone needs to update the public surface.

**Maintained by:** Office of the COO, with section owners listed in each document header.
**Update cadence:** documents are reviewed at least annually, most quarterly. The "Last reviewed" date in each header is canonical.
**Confidentiality:** sections marked *Internal* are not for external distribution; sections marked *Customer-facing* mirror what we publish on smartcartcommerce.com.

## About SmartCartCommerce

A curated, invitation-only digital boutique. We host approximately 380 independent brands, ateliers, and makers across five categories:

1. **Fashion & Apparel** — womenswear, menswear, accessories.
2. **Home & Living** — ceramics, glass, textiles, furniture, kitchen.
3. **Beauty & Wellness** — skincare, fragrance, body, hair.
4. **Artisan Food & Beverage** — chocolate, coffee, oil, honey, cured goods, wine.
5. **Fine Jewelry & Watches** — including authenticated pre-owned.

Headquartered in New York (Tribeca), with offices in Los Angeles, London, and Tokyo. Founded 2019 by Saket Panwar.

About 75% of the brand roster is US-based; the remaining ~25% is select international makers we travel to find — a Kyoto silversmith, an Edinburgh brass artisan, a Provence perfumer — chosen because the work isn't replicated by anyone in the US. SmartCartCommerce ships to 64 countries and is the merchant of record on every order.

For the company narrative, see *01-company/01-company-overview.md* and the founder letter at *01-company/06-founder-letter.md*.

## How to use this repository

**For people who are new to SmartCartCommerce.** Read sections 01 and 02 in full. Skim section 03. The rest you will pick up as your work touches it.

**For concierge.** Sections 02 (customer policies), 03 (care guides), and 05 (support playbooks) are the daily reference. Section 06 helps when customers ask about features. Section 07 (Slack archives) is a useful pattern library.

**For brand partnerships.** Sections 04, 08, and 09 are core. Section 01 should be referenced when explaining how we work to a new brand. Section 09 is the operational reference for the Brand Admin Console — share with brand operators during onboarding.

**For product and engineering.** Section 06 is the customer-facing product surface; sections 04 and 09 are the brand-facing surface; the rest is context that informs decisions.

**For brand operators (your team using the Admin Console).** Start at `09-admin-platform/00-admin-platform-overview.md`. From there, the section relevant to the question you have.

**For new hires across any team.** Read the founder letter (*01-company/06-founder-letter.md*) on your first day. Read the values document (*01-company/02-mission-and-values.md*) on your second.

## Updating documents

Each document has an owner listed in its header. Substantive updates go through that owner. Small corrections (typos, dead links, date refreshes) can be made by anyone with commit access; please add a one-line note in the commit message and notify the owner.

Material policy changes require sign-off from the relevant function lead and a note to the COO. Changes that affect customers materially are emailed to customers thirty days ahead per our terms of service.
