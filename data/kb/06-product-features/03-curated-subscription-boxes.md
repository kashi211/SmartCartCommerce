# Curated Boxes — Subscription

**Document type:** Feature documentation
**Owner:** Saket Panwar
**Last reviewed:** 28 February 2026

---

## What it is

Curated Boxes are themed, multi-brand boxes shipped on a quarterly cadence (March, June, September, December). Each box is built around a theme and includes 4-7 items from across our roster. Themes are not seasonal-pretexts; they are editorial.

Recent themes: *The Studio at Dawn* (Spring 2025), *Things Made With Heat* (Summer 2025), *Quiet Tools* (Autumn 2025), *Letters and Light* (Winter 2025).

Customers do not pick the items in a box. The whole point of the program is the surprise within a defined frame.

## Boxes available

Three programs:

**The Edit** — $260/quarter, $980/year. General — touches multiple categories. ~6 items.

**The Tasting** — $120/quarter. Food & beverage focused. ~5 items.

**The Atelier** — $440/quarter, $1,640/year. Higher value-per-item, often including a single hero piece (a small-format jewelry piece, a candle in a hand-thrown vessel, a textile object). ~5 items.

## Pricing relationship to standalone

Each box totals roughly 1.0–1.1x the equivalent at-retail price of items inside. There is no headline discount. Customers do not subscribe to save money; they subscribe to receive curated discovery.

## Customization

Allowed:

- Allergen flags (food box) — peanut, tree nut, dairy, gluten, etc.
- Fragrance preferences (avoidance only — "no rose," "no oud").
- Color or material avoidances (limited list).
- Skip a quarter.

Not allowed:

- Specific item requests.
- Item swaps after delivery.
- Brand exclusion lists.

We are firm on these because the program's editorial integrity dies the moment we let it become individual personalization.

## What ships in a box

Always:

- A printed booklet from our editorial team explaining the theme, the makers, and individual notes about each item.
- Hand-stitched cotton wrapping for fragile items.
- A handwritten note from the editorial director (we are honest: a printed-handwriting font for now; real handwriting on Atelier-tier).

## Skip and pause

Customers can skip any quarter (announced in advance) or pause indefinitely. Pause does not cancel.

## Cancel

Cancel any time. Pro-rated refund of remaining quarters at the at-retail value of any items already shipped (so cancellation cannot be gamed for arbitrage).

## Returns

Items in a box are returnable individually within the standard 60-day window. Returns of items at the at-retail price are credited normally, not at a "subscription discount."

The booklet, wrapping, and notes are not returnable; we don't ask customers to return them.

## How items are chosen

Editorial team builds each box from a wide consideration set, narrowing through:

- Theme fit (most important).
- Brand rotation (so a single brand doesn't dominate consecutive boxes for the same customer).
- Allergen / preference filters.
- Stock availability (committed 8 weeks out).
- Operational constraints (fragility, shipping zones).

Individual customer's prior boxes inform "do not repeat" — we never send the same item twice.

## Brand participation

Brands are invited to participate in a specific box by editorial. Participation requires committed inventory and a small contribution toward the box's editorial production cost (covered by the Co-op program, see *Marketing Co-op Program*).

Brand selection is editorial. Brands cannot pay to be included.

## Operationally

Boxes ship in a single window — typically the first week of March/June/September/December, in two waves separated by 4 days for fulfillment center capacity. Customers receive ship notification 48 hours before dispatch with an unboxing-window estimate.

Box content reveals are done at the customer level — the booklet tells the story; the website does not show contents until 14 days after the last shipment, to preserve the surprise for customers who haven't received yet.

## Membership and gifting

Subscriptions are giftable as 1-quarter, 2-quarter, or 4-quarter packages. The recipient does not see the gifter's address; the gifter sees a thank-you confirmation when the recipient redeems.

A subscription is not the same as SmartCart Circle. They are separate products. Many Circle members are also Edit subscribers; the platforms compose well.
