# Virtual Showroom

**Document type:** Feature documentation
**Owner:** Saket Panwar
**Last reviewed:** 22 January 2026
**Status:** Generally available, iterating.

---

## What it is

The Virtual Showroom is a guided, scheduled video experience where a customer is walked through a brand's collection — or a curated theme across brands — by a real person at one of our four physical showrooms. It is essentially a video call to a knowledgeable shop, with the customer wandering in and out of camera frames they are interested in.

It is intentionally not a generic browse. Each session is bookable, scheduled, and led by a specific showroom associate.

## Available formats

| Format | Duration | Booking lead time | Who books |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand spotlight | 30 min | 48 hours | Anyone |
| Curated theme | 45 min | 48 hours | Anyone |
| Private session | 60 min | 5 days | Circle members |
| Founder-hosted (occasional) | 60 min | When announced | First-come, first-served |

## What you see

The associate carries a stabilized phone or tablet on a small gimbal. The customer sees the items at scale, in natural light, handled in person, with the associate able to:

- Walk to a different display section.
- Hold an item up close, turn it, show the back, show the inside.
- Compare two items side by side.
- Show fabric drape, weight, behavior under handling.
- Translate sizes by holding two pieces up to compare.

The customer can ask questions in real time. For categories like jewelry and watches, the associate can use a magnifier.

## Why we built it

We sell items where the digital experience falls short of the physical one. A linen shirt photograph cannot show how that linen drapes over a forearm. A diamond photograph cannot show fire. A perfume page cannot show how a fragrance behaves through the day. Virtual Showroom closes a meaningful slice of that gap.

We did not build it as a sales channel. We built it as a help-customer-decide channel. Sales follow naturally, but the metric we watch is customer confidence (post-session survey: "Did you leave with a clearer view of the piece?") not booking-to-purchase conversion.

## Booking and timing

Bookings open 8 weeks out. Slots are released weekly. New York and Tokyo are usually first to fill (timezone-driven scheduling for the US northeast and Asia). Los Angeles and London have more open availability.

We do not over-book. If a slot is taken, it's taken.

## Recording and privacy

Sessions are NOT recorded by default. Customer can request a recording for personal reference; we retain it for 90 days then delete. We do not use session recordings for marketing without explicit signed consent.

Associate-facing notes from the session (what the customer was interested in, any open questions) are written into the customer profile and visible to that customer's concierge.

## Multi-customer and shared sessions

A customer can invite up to 3 additional people to a session — partner, friend, gifting collaborator. The session works the same; one person owns the booking, others can chime in.

## Reschedule and cancel

Free cancel up to 24 hours before the session. Inside 24 hours, we ask for a brief explanation; we don't penalize, but the slot is hard to refill.

## Languages

All four showrooms have at least two associates fluent in non-English languages: New York (EN, ES, FR, JP), Los Angeles (EN, ES, ZH, JP), London (EN, FR, IT, AR), Tokyo (JP, EN, ZH). Custom language pairings on request.

## Customer feedback (selected)

> "I felt like I was actually in the shop. The associate held up the bag against my own bag in the screen frame and we compared sizes. That was the most useful three minutes I've spent shopping online."

> "Saw the watch in three different lights. Made the call easier."

> "It was nice to have someone tell me a piece wasn't right for me before I bought it."

## Internal: associate guidance

Associates are trained on:

- Product knowledge (per category).
- Camera handling and framing.
- Question-asking, listening, customer reading.
- Honest assessment — including saying when an item isn't right.

Associates are evaluated on customer-confidence scores, not on booking-to-purchase conversion. We are explicit about this.
