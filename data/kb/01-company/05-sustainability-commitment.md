# Sustainability Commitment

**Document type:** Public commitment
**Owner:** Saket Panwar
**Last reviewed:** 11 March 2026
**Reporting cadence:** Annual sustainability report, published every September.

---

## What we will not say

We will not call our company "sustainable." Sustainability is a direction, not a state. Any company that calls itself sustainable is asking you to take a marketing claim at face value. We are not going to ask you to do that.

What we will say is: SmartCartCommerce has made a set of specific, measurable commitments, and we publish our progress against them every year, including the years where we did not hit our targets.

## The five commitments

### 1. Carbon — net zero scope 1 & 2 by 2028

Our offices, our showrooms, and our owned fulfillment operations will reach net zero scope 1 and 2 emissions by the end of 2028. We are currently at a 41% reduction against our 2022 baseline. We do not currently make a scope 3 commitment, because the scope 3 footprint of a marketplace is dominated by maker emissions, and dictating maker operations would conflict with our craft-protection principles. We will publish scope 3 *measurement* annually as transparency, not as a target.

### 2. Packaging — fully circular by end of 2026

Every package leaving a SmartCartCommerce fulfillment center will be either home-compostable, curbside-recyclable in the destination market, or returnable for reuse via our prepaid return pouch. As of Q1 2026, we are at 87%. The remaining 13% is custom packaging used by a handful of brands shipping fragile or perishable goods, and we are working through it case by case.

### 3. Take-back — every category, by 2027

Customers can return any qualifying SmartCartCommerce purchase to us at end-of-life. We coordinate repair, resale, donation, or recycling depending on the item. We currently cover Fashion, Home Textiles, and Jewelry. Beauty and Food packaging will join in 2026. Hard goods (ceramics, glass, furniture) will join in 2027.

### 4. Repair — every brand offers a path

Every brand on the platform must offer a repair path for products that can reasonably be repaired. We do not require the maker to repair in-house — we maintain a network of independent restorers — but the path must exist and be documented before listing. This applies retroactively. Brands that cannot meet this requirement have until 31 December 2026 to comply.

### 5. Provenance — verifiable for every claim

If a product page makes a sourcing claim — "single-origin," "GOTS-certified," "responsibly sourced," "recycled silver" — we require verification documentation on file. The documentation does not have to be public. The claim does have to be true.

## Where we have failed

This section exists in every sustainability document at SmartCartCommerce. We add to it.

- **2023:** We promised to remove all virgin plastic from packaging by end-2024. We missed the date by 14 months. The complication was a small group of food brands shipping liquid products in glass bottles for which we had no compliant void-fill. We have since solved this.
- **2024:** We over-stated a recycled-content claim in a holiday campaign. We took it down within 36 hours and ran a follow-up correction. Our director of marketing at the time left the company.
- **2025:** Our ground shipping carbon-offset partner was downgraded by an independent registry. We had not been monitoring registry status closely enough. We have switched providers and added quarterly registry checks.

## What sustainability does not mean here

Sustainability does not mean the most expensive option. It does not mean refusing to ship a chocolate bar across a border because the carbon math is awkward. It does not mean lecturing customers. It means: think hard, be honest about the trade-offs, and choose well, in that order.
