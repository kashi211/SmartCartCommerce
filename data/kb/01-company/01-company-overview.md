## What we are

SmartCartCommerce is a curated, invitation-only digital boutique. We host approximately 380 independent brands, ateliers, and makers across five categories: Womenswear & Menswear, Home & Living, Beauty & Wellness, Artisan Food & Beverage, and Fine Jewelry & Watches. Every brand on our platform has been hand-selected by our curation council. We do not run an open marketplace. We do not accept self-serve applications.

Customers know us as the place to discover the brands their friends haven't heard of yet — small studios in Brooklyn producing twelve handbags a month, a single-origin chocolatier in Asheville, a third-generation woodworker in Vermont, a perfumer working out of a converted Hudson Valley barn. We carry roughly 24,000 SKUs at any given time. Most are made in editions under 500.

About 75% of our brand roster is US-based — concentrated in New York, Los Angeles, the Pacific Northwest, New England, the Hudson Valley, the Carolinas, and Texas, with a long tail across nearly every state. The remaining ~25% is select international makers we travel to find: a Kyoto silversmith, an Edinburgh brass artisan, a Provence perfumer, a Mexico City spice maker, a few others. We carry international work specifically when the craft isn't replicated by anyone in the US — never as a substitute for a comparable American maker.

## Where we are

Headquartered in New York (Tribeca). Operating offices in Los Angeles, London, and Tokyo. Fulfillment is decentralized: brands ship from their own studios — most of them within the continental US — or from one of our four bonded fulfillment partners (New Jersey, Los Angeles, London, Hong Kong). We are a Delaware corporation, with operating subsidiaries in the United Kingdom, Japan, and the European Union for tax and regulatory purposes in those markets.

We ship to 64 countries. Our customers are concentrated in the United States (about 68% of orders), with the next-largest markets being the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Japan, France, and Germany. We are the merchant of record on every order, regardless of where the brand is or where the customer is.

## Who we serve

Our customer is between 32 and 64 years old, household income above $250,000 USD or local equivalent, lives in or visits one of about 40 metropolitan areas, and shops with intent rather than impulse. They will spend nine minutes reading a brand's origin story before deciding to buy a candle. They re-order. They gift. They expect a level of attention from us that mass marketplaces cannot provide and do not attempt.

We do not chase the mass-affluent customer. We do not run flash sales. We do not surface algorithmic recommendations on the homepage.

## How we make money

We take a commission on every sale (variable by category, between 18% and 32%). We earn a small margin on shipping in some regions. We sell a paid membership tier — SmartCart Circle — which contributes roughly 9% of net revenue. We do not sell advertising and we do not accept paid placements on our editorial surfaces. A brand cannot pay to appear on our homepage. Curation is the product.

## How we differ

Three things. First, our editorial layer: every product page carries a perspective written by a member of our curation team, not a marketing description provided by the brand. Second, our concierge: every Circle member has a named human contact who can be reached by message, phone, or in-person at one of our showrooms. Third, our lifecycle: many products on SmartCartCommerce are repairable, refillable, or take-back eligible, and we coordinate that logistics for the customer.

## Tone in one paragraph

SmartCartCommerce speaks the way a knowledgeable friend speaks. We do not chase you with notifications. We do not write in marketing copy. We assume the reader is an intelligent adult with limited time and infinite alternatives. Our job is to be worth their attention.
