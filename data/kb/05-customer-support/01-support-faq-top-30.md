# Customer Support — Top 30 Questions

**Document type:** Internal reference for concierge team + public FAQ source
**Owner:** Saket Panwar
**Last reviewed:** 16 March 2026
**Note:** These are written in the voice we use when responding. They double as our public help center.

---

## Orders

**Q1. When will my order ship?**
A. Most orders ship within 24–72 hours. The product page shows the exact handling time for the item. After dispatch, you'll get a tracking link. If anything is going to slip past the estimate, we'll write to you first.

**Q2. Can I change my shipping address?**
A. Yes, up until the package is on the final-mile vehicle. Use *Edit shipping* in your account, or message us. International orders move faster through customs, so the window is narrower; we'll do our best.

**Q3. Can I cancel my order?**
A. Yes — any time before it ships. After it ships, the return process applies. For made-to-order, see the order cancellation policy.

**Q4. Why are some items shipped separately?**
A. Most items ship from the maker's own studio. If you order from three brands, you'll receive three packages. The exception is when items are held in our fulfillment centers and consolidate naturally.

**Q5. Where's my order?**
A. Tracking is in your account or in your shipping confirmation email. If the tracker has been silent for more than 72 hours, message us — we'll pull a private status from the carrier.

## Returns & refunds

**Q6. What's your return policy?**
A. 60 days from delivery for most items. Free returns in our home markets, discounted elsewhere. Some categories (perishables, opened beauty, personalized jewelry) have exceptions — see *Returns & Refunds*.

**Q7. How do I start a return?**
A. From your account, click *Start a return* on the order. You'll receive a label by email within 30 minutes. Or reply to your order confirmation with the word "return."

**Q8. When will I see my refund?**
A. Within 1 business day of us receiving the return at our facility. Funds typically appear on your card within 3–5 business days after that.

**Q9. Can I exchange for a different size?**
A. Yes — and it's our preferred outcome. Free exchange shipping in our home markets. Use the same flow; choose *Exchange* instead of *Return*.

**Q10. Do you charge restocking fees?**
A. No.

## Damage & quality

**Q11. My item arrived damaged. What now?**
A. Photos to *concierge@smartcartcommerce.com* within 14 days. We replace, repair, or refund — your call. We don't require unboxing video.

**Q12. My item broke after a few months. Is it covered?**
A. Probably. See our Care Promise: 12 months on fashion, 5 years on furniture frames, lifetime on fine jewelry, etc. Send photos and we'll make it right.

**Q13. The color looks different than the photo. Can I return it?**
A. Yes, within 60 days, no fuss. Color reproduction varies with screens; we'd rather have an honest customer than a frustrated one.

## Sizing & fit

**Q14. What size should I order?**
A. Use the flat measurements on the product page. If you've stored your measurements, the page will show *Likely fits / Doesn't fit*. Concierge can also help — we have access to fit history by SKU.

**Q15. Can I send my measurements for help?**
A. Yes — message us at *concierge@smartcartcommerce.com* or, if you're a Circle member, your concierge directly.

**Q16. What if I'm between sizes?**
A. Most fashion brands tend toward the smaller of the two. Knitwear: size up if you prefer relaxed. Tailoring: size down if you'll have it altered. Concierge can give specifics by brand.

## International orders

**Q17. Will I have to pay customs?**
A. No. We ship Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) — duties and taxes are included at checkout. No surprise bills.

**Q18. Why isn't my country at checkout?**
A. We currently don't ship to a small set of markets due to sanctions or carrier reliability. The list is in our *International Shipping Guide*.

**Q19. Why is shipping slow to my country?**
A. Long distance, sometimes border processing. Express options are available. SmartCart Circle members ship free on standard with no minimum.

## Account & payment

**Q20. Why was my card declined?**
A. Most often, your bank flagged a foreign-merchant transaction. Your bank will lift the flag if you confirm. Otherwise, try Apple Pay / Google Pay, which often clear without re-confirmation.

**Q21. Do you store my card?**
A. We don't store full card numbers. Our payment processors (Stripe, Adyen) hold a tokenized reference for one-click checkout if you opt in.

**Q22. How do I close my account?**
A. From the account page, *Close account*. Or email us. We delete what we're not legally required to retain.

## SmartCart Circle

**Q23. What's SmartCart Circle?**
A. Our paid annual membership: named concierge, free shipping & returns, early access, members-only items, repair credit, quarterly journal. $240/yr or local equivalent.

**Q24. Do points or rewards exist?**
A. No. We don't run a points system. Circle is one fee, one set of benefits.

**Q25. Can I cancel Circle?**
A. Yes, any time. Pro-rata refund for the unused portion if cancelling mid-year.

## Gift cards

**Q26. How long are gift cards valid?**
A. 10 years.

**Q27. Can I use a gift card with a Circle membership?**
A. No — we ask Circle to be funded by the member directly. You can buy a Circle membership *as* a gift, however.

## Other

**Q28. Do you offer gift wrapping?**
A. Yes, on most items, and it's free. Add a note at checkout.

**Q29. Can I send something to a different address than my own?**
A. Yes, set a different shipping address at checkout. Gift orders include a packing slip without prices.

**Q30. How do I contact a real person?**
A. Email *concierge@smartcartcommerce.com*. Live chat available in your account during business hours. SmartCart Circle members can call their concierge directly.
