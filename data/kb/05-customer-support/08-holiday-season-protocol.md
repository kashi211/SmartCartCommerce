# Holiday Season Protocol

**Document type:** Internal — operations
**Owner:** Saket Panwar
**Last reviewed:** 14 March 2026
**Applicable to:** 01 November–06 January each year.

---

## Why we have a separate protocol

The November–early-January window represents about 31% of our annual order volume in 24% of the year, with concentration around three peaks: Black Friday weekend, "last guaranteed shipping" deadlines, and the week between Christmas and New Year. Without specific operational changes, our normal service standards would degrade. We have invested in not letting that happen.

We do not run flash sales or Cyber Monday discounts. The volume increase is driven by gifting demand, not by promotional pricing.

## Staffing

Concierge headcount roughly doubles between 01 November and 31 December. Two paths:

1. **Returning seasonal team** — about 60% of seasonal hires return year over year. We start outreach in August.
2. **New seasonal team** — onboarded September–October with a 5-week training that covers full policy and our voice.

Brand-specific specialists (jewelry, watches, fine food) staff additional shifts during the period.

## Service-level commitments — adjusted

The standard SLAs hold, with two adjustments published in advance:

- Standard concierge response: < 90 minutes business hours, vs. < 90 minutes 24/7 the rest of the year.
- Circle concierge response: < 30 minutes during peak (08:00-22:00 in their time zone).

We publish these on the help center the first week of November so customers know what to expect.

## Holiday-specific shipping deadlines

We publish "order by" deadlines per market for guaranteed pre-Christmas delivery. Conservatively. We pad by 2 business days against carrier guarantees.

Sample 2026 deadlines (subject to update):

| Market | Standard order-by | Express order-by |
|---|---|---|
| US continental | Dec 13 | Dec 18 |
| UK & Ireland | Dec 14 | Dec 20 |
| EU Schengen | Dec 13 | Dec 19 |
| Australia / NZ | Dec 8 | Dec 15 |
| Japan | Dec 12 | Dec 18 |

Made-to-order items have brand-specific deadlines posted on the product page. We do not make blanket "before Christmas" promises on made-to-order during the season.

## Inventory

A buffer stock policy applies for high-velocity SKUs — typically 1.4x normal hold against forecast for items above a certain volume threshold. Brands are asked to commit to buffer levels by 15 September. The Co-op covers half of any overstock cost if a buffer doesn't sell through; this is a long-standing arrangement that gives brands confidence to commit.

## Gifting

Gifting volume increases sharply. We add three gifting-specific capabilities for the period:

- **Late-night gift wrap.** Free gift wrap continues. Showroom team adds a late-night wrap shift in the final week.
- **Gift card escalation.** Last-minute digital gift cards remain available right up until Dec 24, 23:59 in the recipient's time zone.
- **Concierge gift advice.** A "Gift Help" line opens in early November, available to anyone (not just Circle members) for personalized gift selection. ~3,200 sessions in 2025.

## Returns surge

Post-holiday return volume is concentrated in the first three weeks of January. We extend the return window for items purchased between Nov 15 and Dec 25 — those items have until Feb 28 to return, instead of the standard 60 days. The extended window is communicated at purchase and on packing slips.

## Brand communications

We commit to brands that:

- Payouts run on the normal cadence (no holiday shifts).
- Order volume forecasts are shared weekly during the period.
- Quality investigations are not initiated for one-off issues during the peak; we investigate post-season.
- Account leads remain available; QBRs are paused for the season unless brand-requested.

## Office closures

SmartCartCommerce offices close: Dec 24-26 (NY + LA), Dec 24-27 (London), Dec 31 afternoon, Jan 1 (full). Tokyo follows local convention. Concierge coverage continues through all of these days at reduced staffing, with US team on Eastern + Pacific shifts.

Brands that ship from their own studios should publish their closure schedule by Nov 15 so customer-facing estimates can be adjusted.

## Stress signals

Internal stress markers we watch:

- Concierge response time creeping above 90-min threshold.
- Same-day cancellation rate from brands above 1% (vs. < 0.4% normally).
- Damage rate above 1.5% during peak.
- NPS dropping more than 3 points in any week.

If any signal trips, the holiday war room (cross-functional, daily 9am call from Nov 20) decides intervention.

## Post-mortem

A formal post-season review runs in late January. Findings shape next-year staffing, deadlines, brand communication, and the Co-op program.
