## Welcome

If you are reading this, your brand has been accepted to SmartCartCommerce. The acceptance was not casual. Our curation council has reviewed your work, your studio, your materials, and your story. We are committed to being a serious channel for you. This handbook describes how we work together for the first 120 days and beyond.

## The 90-day onboarding arc

We aim to take a new brand from signed agreement to live-on-platform within 90 days. Some brands move faster; some move slower. We do not push past readiness for an arbitrary date.

| Week | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 0 | Signed agreement, kickoff call with your account lead |
| 1–2 | Tax & banking setup, brand info form, image library upload |
| 3–4 | Editorial visit (onsite where feasible, video where not) |
| 5–6 | Listing draft, photo audit, copy review |
| 7–8 | Inventory sync, fulfillment connection, sample shipping test |
| 9–10 | Soft launch — listings visible to internal QA only |
| 11–12 | Public launch + first concierge briefing |
| 13+ | Ongoing relationship cadence (see below) |

## Your account lead

Every brand has a single named account lead at SmartCartCommerce. They are your primary contact for the duration of your time on the platform. They are not a salesperson; they are your operator. They know your inventory, your seasonality, your team, and your priorities.

Account lead responsibilities: weekly check-in during onboarding, biweekly thereafter, quarterly business review, advocacy inside SmartCartCommerce when something needs to move.

## What we ask of you

We ask three things of every brand.

**Be honest.** About your stock, your lead times, your materials, your labor, your story. We will catch what's off, and we'd much rather not catch it.

**Be responsive.** Within 24 hours during business days for routine items, 4 hours for customer-impacting issues. Slow responsiveness is the most common reason a brand falls behind on the platform.

**Be willing to be edited.** Our editorial team will write your product copy. We'll show you drafts. You can flag inaccuracies. You cannot, however, rewrite our editorial voice — that is the point of the platform. We have not had a brand fail this principle in a way that ended the relationship.

## What we promise you

**A real audience.** Our customer is high-intent and well-resourced. We will not flood you with low-quality traffic.

**Real money on time.** Payouts twice a month, on the 7th and 22nd. Funds settle within 2 business days of those dates. We have not missed a payout cycle in five years.

**Curation as a moat.** Our curation is what protects you on the platform. We do not water it down for revenue. You are not competing with thirty other versions of yourself.

**No dropshippers, no resellers, no diluted listings.** Your brand will not appear next to a knockoff because we list direct from the maker.

**Real merchandising support.** Editorial features, gift-guide inclusion, journal coverage, and marketing email placements are made on editorial merit. We do not charge for placement. We do not accept payment for placement.

## Communication channels

- **Routine ops:** the Brand Partner portal (your dashboard) for orders, inventory, payouts, support tickets.
- **Account-level conversations:** Slack Connect channel with your account lead (available on request).
- **Issues:** *partners@smartcartcommerce.com*, monitored 24/7.
- **Quarterly business review:** scheduled in advance, in person where possible.

## What happens when something goes wrong

We treat brand-side mistakes (a stockout, a delayed shipment, a quality issue) as collaboration moments. We absorb the customer-facing cost first (concierge handling, replacement, refund), then we look at root cause with you afterward, then we decide together what to change.

We do not penalize automatically. We do not deactivate listings without conversation. We do not surprise.
