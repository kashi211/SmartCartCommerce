## Commission rates by category

Commissions are negotiated at signing within these published bands. We do not run special rates for investor-introduced brands or favored partners. Bands are reviewed annually.

| Category | Standard band | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Womenswear & Menswear | 24%–32% | Lower band for established brands; higher for full editorial support |
| Home & Living (textiles) | 22%–30% | Same |
| Home & Living (furniture) | 18%–24% | Lower because freight handling is brand-side |
| Beauty & Wellness | 26%–34% | Higher because regulatory and marketing investment is significant |
| Artisan Food & Beverage | 22%–28% | Lower band for cold-chain participants |
| Fine Jewelry & Watches | 18%–26% | Lower because authentication and aftercare are revenue-shared |

Commissions apply to the merchandise total only. They do not apply to shipping fees, gift-wrap upcharges, or sales tax / VAT.

## What's in the commission

The commission funds: marketplace operations, payment processing fees, fraud insurance, editorial production, photography (where commissioned), customer support, concierge, returns logistics, take-back program, technology platform, and marketing investment in the brand.

We are deliberately transparent about this. Brands sometimes negotiate down on the assumption the rate is profit margin. It is not — payment processing alone is 2.4–3.2%, fraud insurance is another 0.6%, and concierge represents about 4 points of effective rate at our current volume.

## Payout cadence

We pay out twice monthly:

- **The 7th** of each month — covering net sales from the 16th to the end of the prior month.
- **The 22nd** of each month — covering net sales from the 1st to the 15th of the current month.

Funds settle in your bank account within 2 business days of those dates. SEPA settlements are typically same-day.

## Currency

Payouts are made in your business's home currency by default. We support: USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CHF, AUD, NZD, SGD, HKD, SEK, NOK, DKK, CAD. Currency conversion (where applicable) uses the prior business day's mid-market rate plus a 0.4% spread, capped at the spread Stripe charges us. We pass through the actual rate.

## Net of returns

Returns are netted against the commission cycle in which they are processed. If a customer returns an order from a previous cycle, the corresponding commission is reversed in the current cycle.

If a brand carries a high return rate (>9% category-adjusted), we have an early-conversation policy: account lead surfaces the trend, we explore root cause together, no automatic deduction.

## Chargebacks

Chargebacks land on SmartCartCommerce, not the brand. We absorb chargeback risk fully. The exception is verified brand-side fraud (e.g., misrepresented inventory, "ghost" orders), which is governed by the seller agreement.

## Holds

We may temporarily hold a portion of payout in two cases:

1. **Unresolved disputes** — a specific order under investigation. Hold is order-level only.
2. **Account flags** — fraud signals, suspected stolen-goods listings, or regulatory holds (e.g., a customs investigation). We notify you within 24 hours of any such hold.

We do not hold a "rolling reserve" on any partner. We pay net and we pay regularly.

## Statements

You receive a statement on every payout date showing: gross sales, returns, commission, payment processing fees passed-through (where applicable), shipping pass-through, currency conversion, and the net wire amount. The statement is downloadable as PDF and as CSV for your accounting system.

## Tax

We provide a 1099-K equivalent annually for US brands, and equivalent regulatory documents for EU, UK, and APAC brands. We do not provide tax advice. Talk to your accountant.

## Disputes about payouts

Email *finance-disputes@smartcartcommerce.com* with the statement reference. We commit to a written response within 5 business days. Most disputes resolve within 10.
