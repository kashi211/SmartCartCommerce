# Photography & Listing Standards

**Document type:** Brand-facing standard
**Owner:** Saket Panwar
**Last reviewed:** 09 March 2026

---

## The look of the platform

SmartCartCommerce has a recognizable visual signature. It is quiet. It uses natural light. It uses negative space. It does not use heavy retouching, lifestyle props that distract, or color treatments that alter the actual color of the product.

Brand listings on SmartCartCommerce must respect this signature. We are happy to help any brand meet it; we will not allow listings that violate it.

## What we accept from brands

Every product needs a minimum of:

1. **One front-facing primary image** — product clearly framed, neutral background (white, off-white, soft natural).
2. **Two to four supplementary images** — detail, scale, alternate angle, "in use" or "in context" where appropriate.
3. **One on-figure or in-situ image** for fashion, jewelry, and home goods — where the item is being worn or placed in a setting.

Resolution: 2400 px on the long edge, minimum. We accept up to 6000 px and will display efficiently for any device.

Color profile: sRGB. Output as JPEG (quality ≥85) or PNG.

File naming convention: `{brand-slug}_{sku}_{shot-type}.jpg` — e.g., `riva-marin_RM-018_primary.jpg`. We will rename if needed; following the convention speeds your onboarding.

## What we reject

- **Heavy retouching.** Skin smoothed beyond reality; ceramic glaze digitally evened; a wood grain replaced. The product should look the way it actually looks.
- **Background-replacement artifacts.** Halos, missing shadows, missing reflections. We can re-shoot or re-cut for you if the brand image is otherwise great.
- **Lifestyle stock photography.** A garment hung on a coat-hook in a "lifestyle" setting that has nothing to do with the brand. We want context that is real.
- **Watermarks of any kind.**
- **Color treatments unfaithful to the actual product.** Bumped saturation, warmed-up white balance to make a beige look sandy when it's actually grey.

## Editorial photography

For most brands, our editorial team commissions one photoshoot per year — usually around a seasonal launch or a brand profile feature. Editorial photography is shot by our team or commissioned partners, fully owned by SmartCartCommerce, and licensed to the brand for non-commercial use. Brands cannot use editorial photography for paid advertising without our written approval.

## Copy standards

Product copy on SmartCartCommerce is written or substantially edited by our editorial team. We start from the brand's submission, fact-check it, and rewrite for our voice (see *Brand Voice & Tone Guidelines*). The brand reviews and signs off on the final copy before listing goes live.

Every product has:

- **Title** — short, descriptive, no superlatives ("Riva linen tunic" not "Best-selling Riva linen luxury tunic").
- **Primary description** (140–220 words) — what it is, who made it, what makes it specific, how it behaves.
- **Care, materials, sizing.** Fact-checked, complete, in the standard SmartCartCommerce format.
- **Origin & maker note** (40–80 words) — a few sentences about who made this and where.

We do not allow:

- Pricing claims ("retail price was…").
- Comparative claims against other brands.
- Health, anti-aging, or medical claims (Beauty in particular).
- Urgency cues ("almost gone," "won't last," "selling fast"). We do show stock counts on truly limited pieces.

## Inclusive imagery

Our model and lifestyle imagery represents a wide range of body types, ages, ethnicities, and gender expressions. We expect brand-supplied imagery to reflect the same standard. Where it doesn't, we may request reshoots or supplement with our own.

## Accessibility

Every image has a meaningful alt text written by our editorial team. Brands do not need to supply alt text — but if you have it, share it.

## Audit

We re-audit brand listings every 12 months. Listings that have drifted (changed materials, changed pricing structure, photography updates) are refreshed in collaboration with the brand. We do not silently change or downgrade listings.
