# Seller Dispute Resolution

**Document type:** Brand-facing process
**Owner:** Saket Panwar
**Last reviewed:** 19 February 2026

---

## What a dispute looks like

There are five common types of disputes between SmartCartCommerce and a brand:

1. **Payout disputes** — a brand believes they have been underpaid, miscalculated, or held without cause.
2. **Returns / refund disputes** — a brand disagrees that a particular return was eligible.
3. **Quality investigations** — SmartCartCommerce has flagged an item for a perceived quality issue and the brand disagrees.
4. **Authenticity or labor disputes** — a sourcing claim or labor declaration is challenged.
5. **Off-boarding disputes** — a brand contests a delisting decision.

We have a single process for all five. It has three stages, each designed to resolve at the lowest level possible.

## Stage 1 — Direct resolution

90% of disputes resolve here.

The brand's account lead is the first point of contact. The brand emails the lead or files a ticket via the Partner Portal with a description of the dispute, the order or statement reference, and any supporting documentation.

The account lead acknowledges within 1 business day, investigates, and returns a written response within 5 business days. If facts are clear, the lead has full authority to resolve without escalation: refund, payout adjustment, listing reinstatement, etc.

## Stage 2 — Operations review

If the brand and the account lead cannot agree, the matter goes to Marketplace Operations leadership. This stage adds an independent reviewer who has not been involved in the original interaction.

The reviewer:

- Pulls the full order, communication, and decision history.
- Re-reviews the seller agreement clauses applicable.
- Speaks with the brand and the account lead independently.
- Issues a written ruling within 10 business days.

## Stage 3 — Resolution panel

For matters not resolved at Stage 2, the brand can request a Resolution Panel. The panel consists of:

- One SmartCartCommerce executive (rotating; cannot be the executive responsible for the original decision).
- One member of our independent advisory board (rotates from a panel of six external advisors — retailers, lawyers, sustainability specialists).
- One peer brand representative (drawn from a standing pool of partner brands who have agreed to serve, paid an honorarium per case).

The Resolution Panel meets within 21 days of request. They issue a binding decision within 7 days of meeting.

This process is unusual for marketplaces. We adopted it because the alternative — final-word-from-SmartCartCommerce — is not a fair pattern for the brands we partner with.

## What's protected during a dispute

While a dispute is open:

- We do not delist the brand.
- We do not withhold payouts on undisputed orders.
- We do not communicate about the dispute to third parties.
- We continue normal operations — orders ship, customers are served.

The exception is matters involving immediate consumer safety (e.g., contamination, allergen mislabeling, fraud), where we will pause sales of affected SKUs until the matter is resolved.

## Documentation

Every stage produces a written record. The brand receives copies of all written records at each stage. We retain them for 7 years.

If the brand and SmartCartCommerce ultimately part ways, the dispute record remains confidential. We do not publish, share with other marketplaces, or use it in any external context.

## External recourse

This process does not limit any rights the brand has under their seller agreement or under applicable law. If a brand prefers to skip the internal process and proceed under the agreement's governing law, they can. Most brands choose the internal process because it is faster and lower cost.

## Patterns we have observed

Reviewing five years of dispute history:

- The most common dispute is a return-eligibility disagreement. Outcome: ~75% of the time, the customer's interpretation prevails on review.
- Payout disputes are usually about timing or currency conversion. Outcome: 90%+ resolve at Stage 1.
- Authenticity disputes are rare but serious. Outcome: panels have ruled both ways.
- Off-boarding disputes are very rare. We work hard to make off-boarding mutual; surprise off-boarding is not a thing we do.

## A note on tone

Disputes are uncomfortable. We try to be careful about language, even in writing. If a brand-facing reply ever reads as defensive, dismissive, or boilerplate, that is not a SmartCartCommerce-style response and should be flagged to the brand's account lead.
