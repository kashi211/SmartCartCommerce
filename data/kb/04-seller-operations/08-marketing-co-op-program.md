**Document type:** Brand-facing program description
**Owner:** Saket Panwar

## What it is, what it isn't

The SmartCartCommerce Marketing Co-op is the only paid marketing program we offer brands. It is opt-in, transparent, and decoupled from editorial decisions. Brands cannot pay to appear in editorial. Brands cannot pay to rank in search. Brands cannot pay for homepage placement. The Co-op funds *expansion* activities — new geographies, sample-volume kits, brand-introduction events — that benefit both sides and require shared investment.

We talk about this distinction directly because confusing the two is exactly how editorial integrity dies on platforms like ours.

## What's funded by Co-op

| Activity | SmartCartCommerce share | Brand share |
|---|---|---|
| Geographic expansion campaign (new market) | 60% | 40% |
| Discovery sample kit (multi-brand sampler box) | 50% | 50% |
| Showroom event (New York, Los Angeles, London, Tokyo) | 70% | 30% |
| Editorial-style brand profile (paid version) | NOT AVAILABLE | NOT AVAILABLE |
| Search ranking | NOT AVAILABLE | NOT AVAILABLE |
| Homepage placement | NOT AVAILABLE | NOT AVAILABLE |

The third row is hard. We get asked about it. We say no.

## Geographic expansion campaign

When a brand wants to launch into a new market we already serve, the Co-op funds a structured campaign:

- Localized email feature in the destination market's newsletter.
- Editorial introduction (written by us, free of charge — this is editorial, not paid).
- A 4-week boost in destination-market merchandising slots that the brand qualifies for under normal editorial criteria.
- Campaign-specific metrics dashboard for the brand.

Cost: $4,500 USD for the brand share, typically. Final figure varies with market and scale.

Outcome we expect: 20–40% lift in destination-market revenue during the campaign window. We share results transparently after.

## Discovery sample kit

Multi-brand samplers are a quarterly box of small-format products from a curated set of brands, sold to customers as a "discover" purchase or gifted to existing high-affinity customers.

The brand provides product at cost (or a small margin); SmartCartCommerce handles assembly, packaging, fulfillment, and editorial. Brand share covers physical product and packaging-side branding. SmartCartCommerce share covers everything else.

Brands joining a sample kit are not selected by paying — they are selected editorially. The Co-op contribution funds the operational cost. We are explicit about this with brands considering the kit.

## Showroom events

A showroom event is an in-person evening at one of our four physical spaces, featuring 1-3 brands. Includes invitations to Circle members, a curated tasting or styling session, and sometimes a press preview.

SmartCartCommerce handles event design, staff, food and beverage, RSVPs, and post-event editorial. Brand handles travel for the founder or representative, sample inventory, and a contribution toward variable costs.

Showroom events are scheduled approximately 12 months out. We host roughly 18-24 per year across all locations.

## What we will not do

A few things brands sometimes ask us to fund. We will not:

- Run third-party paid ads featuring a single brand on a CPM/CPC basis.
- Co-fund a brand's own paid advertising on Meta, Google, etc.
- Promote a brand on our owned channels for cash (this is the editorial line).
- Permit "boost" payments to lift a product in algorithmic placements.
- Allow brand-supplied editorial copy to publish under a SmartCartCommerce editorial byline.

The reason is consistent: those tactics would degrade the platform asset that makes it valuable to the brand in the first place.

## How to participate

Bring it up with your account lead at QBR or any time. The lead works with our marketing team to shape the proposal. Average lead time from "interested" to "live" is 6-10 weeks for an expansion campaign, 12+ weeks for a showroom event.

Approval requires both account lead and marketing director sign-off.

## Reporting

Every Co-op campaign comes with a post-mortem written by our marketing team within 30 days of campaign end. The post-mortem includes: actual spend by line item, attribution of revenue lift (with method clearly described), customer feedback themes, brand sentiment from the destination market, and recommendations for future activity.

Post-mortems are honest. We have written negative ones — campaigns that underperformed our model.
