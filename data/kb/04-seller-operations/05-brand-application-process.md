# Brand Application Process

**Document type:** External / brand-facing
**Owner:** Saket Panwar
**Last reviewed:** 02 February 2026

---

## How brands join SmartCartCommerce

Brands do not apply through a public form. We accept new partners through four pathways. Each maps to a different way we discover work we believe in.

### 1. Scout-led

Members of our curation team travel. They visit studios, fairs, ateliers, design weeks, craft villages, food festivals, jewelry shows. Around 60% of our roster came in this way. If you have been visited by someone with a small notebook and a curious set of questions, that was us.

### 2. Nominated

Existing SmartCartCommerce brands and trusted retail partners can nominate other makers. Nominations skip the cold-outreach queue and go to a first-call within 21 days. Roughly 18% of our roster came in by nomination.

### 3. Open call

Twice a year — March (emerging makers) and September (international expansion priority countries) — we open public submissions. The form is short. The review takes 6–10 weeks. We typically receive 1,800–2,400 submissions per cycle and accept around 12–18. Roughly 14% of our roster has come in this way.

### 4. Inherited

We acquired Le Tempietto, a small competitor focused on Italian artisan home goods, in 2023. We re-curated its roster and inherited the partners who passed our seven gates. Roughly 8% of our current roster came from that acquisition. We are not currently planning further acquisitions.

## What we look for

See *Curation Standards* for the full seven-gate review. In summary:

- Identifiable maker.
- Made, not assembled.
- Honest materials.
- Defensible pricing.
- True story.
- Paid labor.
- Adds something we don't have.

We also pay attention to a brand's operational readiness. A wonderful brand that ships from a leaky garage with no inventory system can still join us — we will help build the operational layer. We just need to know the work upfront.

## Timeline expectations

| Stage | Typical duration |
|---|---|
| First contact to first call | 1–4 weeks |
| First call to studio visit | 2–6 weeks |
| Studio visit to internal review | 1–2 weeks |
| Internal review to decision | 2–4 weeks |
| Decision to onboarding kickoff | 1 week |
| Onboarding to first listing | 8–12 weeks (see Onboarding Handbook) |

End-to-end: 90–180 days. We are deliberately not faster.

## What you'll be asked

In the first call we ask about your story (founders, founding year, why this work), your team (who makes what; who handles operations), your supply chain (where materials come from; who you work with downstream), your pricing rationale, your other channels (boutiques, your own site, other marketplaces), your inventory practices, and your appetite for editorial collaboration.

We do not ask for revenue figures upfront. We will ask, gently, about scale during onboarding so we can plan inventory and merchandising appropriately.

## Documents we'll request

After verbal interest is mutual:

- Business registration / VAT documents.
- A signed labor declaration.
- Material sourcing documents for any specific claim (organic, GOTS, GRS, recycled silver, single-origin, etc.).
- Brand book or look-and-feel document, if you have one.
- High-resolution image library or FTP/Dropbox access.

## Why we say no

Most brands we say no to are simply duplicative — wonderful work that overlaps with a brand we already carry. We try to say no clearly and quickly so the maker can move on. We do not say no by silence.

Other common reasons: pricing not defensible against materials and labor; story claims that don't hold up to verification; supply chain not transparent enough for our standard; brand not operationally ready and not interested in becoming so.

## If we say no, can you re-apply?

Yes. There is no quiet "no list." Brands evolve, we evolve. We have accepted brands on second or third approach. We recommend a 12-month gap between approaches and a clear signal of what has changed.
