# Tax Configuration

**Document type:** Brand-facing reference + platform standard
**Owner:** Saket Panwar
**Last reviewed:** 12 March 2026

---

## In one paragraph

Tax — sales tax, VAT, GST, and equivalents — is handled by SmartCartCommerce at the platform level. We are the merchant of record on every order; we calculate the right tax based on the customer's destination, collect it, and remit it to the relevant tax authority. Brands do not register for tax in markets they ship into via SmartCartCommerce. This is one of the largest operational benefits of being on the platform — the alternative is registering and remitting in dozens of jurisdictions yourself.

## How tax flows

1. Customer enters shipping address at checkout.
2. We compute landed cost: item price + shipping + applicable tax.
3. Customer sees the total clearly. Tax line is itemized.
4. At order capture, we collect everything — including tax.
5. At payout time, your portion is the merchandise total minus commission. Tax is held by us and remitted to authorities on the appropriate cadence.
6. Tax invoices and customer receipts are generated by us.

For other markets, we handle on a case-by-case basis with tax counsel, including cases where economic nexus has been triggered.

## Tax tables

The tax-rate database is platform-managed and updated continuously. Sources include:

- Streamlined Sales Tax governing board (US).
- HMRC, EU Commission, individual EU member-state authorities.
- National tax authorities for each registered jurisdiction.

We use Avalara as our authoritative tax-rate engine. We don't ask brands to maintain rate tables; we maintain it for the platform.

## Tax categories

Some products tax differently within a market:

- Apparel in some US states (PA, NJ): exempt from sales tax.
- Beauty and cosmetics: standard rate in most markets.
- Food: often reduced-rate or exempt in EU and UK; varies in US.
- Children's items: lower rate in some EU markets.

Each product on SmartCartCommerce has a tax category set at listing time. We assign the right code (Avalara TaxCode + local equivalent). For ambiguous cases, our tax team reviews. Brands don't configure tax categories themselves; if a product is mis-categorized, contact your account lead.

## Tax Calculations

Inclusive vs exclusive display:

- **EU and UK:** prices shown inclusive of VAT, per consumer-protection law.
- **US, Canada, AU/NZ:** prices shown exclusive; tax added at checkout.
- **Japan:** inclusive (consumption tax).
- **Other markets:** per local convention.

The customer sees a clear tax line on receipt regardless. Adobe of where tax is or isn't included in the headline price.

## Tax Tables (brand-facing view)

In the Admin Console at *Orders → Tax → Tables*, brands see a read-only summary of:

- The markets we collect tax in.
- The current rates for your product mix.
- Recent rate changes.

You cannot edit. The view is provided so you can answer customer questions ("Why is the tax 19% on my Berlin order?") with reference to the rate.

## Core Options (brand-facing)

A small panel at *Orders → Tax → Options*. Read-only for brands; visible so you understand the platform decisions.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Tax-inclusive markets | EU, UK, JP, others per convention |
| Tax-exclusive markets | US, CA, AU, NZ |
| Round at line | Yes (standard) |
| Round at order | No |
| Tax on shipping | Per market (yes in EU/UK; varies in US) |
| Tax on gift wrap | Per market (yes where gift wrap is taxable) |
| Tax on gift cards | No (collected at redemption) |

## Reporting

Tax reporting in *Analytics → Tax*:

- Tax collected by jurisdiction by period.
- Tax remitted (with confirmation references).
- Annual sales by jurisdiction (for your tax records).

You can export tax-detail reports for your accountant. The platform also provides annual tax summary documents at year-end.

## What brands need to know

- **Don't double-tax.** Don't add tax to your prices outside the platform; we add tax at checkout. If you set a tax-inclusive price thinking you're handling EU VAT, the customer would be charged VAT on top.
- **Don't claim platform-collected tax as your own revenue.** Your revenue is the merchandise total. Tax collected is held in trust and remitted to authorities.
- **Reach out for unusual cases.** A product that should tax at a reduced rate but shows standard rate; a market we don't yet collect in but you have nexus in for other reasons; a customer asking for a tax-exempt purchase. Email *tax@smartcartcommerce.com* via your account lead.

## Tax-exempt customers

Some customers are tax-exempt in their jurisdiction (resale buyers, charitable organizations, government entities). The customer can submit exemption documentation through their account; concierge or our tax team verifies; the customer's future orders proceed without tax collection.

You will see *Tax-exempt* tagged on the order in your queue. The customer's exemption status is platform-verified; you don't need to do anything special.

## Tax invoices

We issue a customer-facing tax invoice for every order, in compliance with the destination market's invoicing standards. The invoice includes:

- Our (SCC's) tax registration number where required.
- Order date.
- Itemized line items with applied tax.
- Tax amount per line.
- Any tax-exempt categorization.

Customers can re-download invoices from their account. For accounting customers (B2B / wholesale), the invoice format complies with regional B2B requirements.

## What we don't handle

- **Brand income tax.** You are responsible for income tax on your earnings. Your 1099-K equivalent (or local equivalent) is provided annually.
- **Customs duties.** Treated separately from tax. See the *International Shipping Guide* for Delivered Duty Paid handling.
- **Resale/wholesale tax obligations.** If your brand operates a wholesale program outside SmartCartCommerce, your tax compliance for those channels is yours.
