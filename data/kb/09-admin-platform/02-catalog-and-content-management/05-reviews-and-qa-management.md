## Scope

This document covers the operator tooling: how to read, reply to, search, export, and configure your brand's review and Q&A surfaces inside the Admin Console.

Policy questions (verified-buyer requirements, what gets removed, anti-manipulation) live in the platform-policy doc.

## Reviews — viewing and responding

*Catalog → Reviews.*

The default view is *Pending response* — reviews in the last 14 days that haven't received a brand reply, sorted oldest first. Tabs: Pending response, All reviews, Search, Options.

For each review you see: customer first name, star rating across all dimensions, written content, photos (if attached), order date, and a *Respond* button.

### Replying

Replies are public on the product page. They follow our standards:

- Plain language, in your brand voice (which is your voice; you don't have to match SmartCartCommerce's editorial voice in your replies).
- No personally identifying information about the reviewer beyond the first name they chose to display.
- No offers (discount codes, "we'll make it right with a coupon"). If you want to follow up with the customer privately, ask concierge to relay; we'll handle it through the platform's customer relationship.
- No defensive boilerplate ("we're sorry to hear that you didn't have a perfect experience"). Either say something real or stay quiet.

We require a reply within 14 days for any review surfacing a substantive product issue (defect, sizing, breakage). For routine reviews (positive or mildly negative), reply if you have something to add; otherwise it is fine to be quiet.

### Search reviews

A separate tab with full-text search across all your reviews. Filters: date range, rating, fulfillment center, customer-tier (Circle vs non-Circle), keyword.

### Pending Reviews

Reviews flagged by our trust system for human review (suspected spam, suspected boilerplate, suspected coordinated reviews). Most resolve within 24 hours. While in this state they are not displayed publicly. You see them with a flag icon; you cannot publish them yourself.

### Product Review Export

CSV of all your reviews. Useful for analysis, for sharing with your manufacturing partner, or for your own records. Export at *Reviews → Export*.

## Q&A — viewing and responding

Customer-asked questions about your products, public on the product page once answered. *Catalog → Q&A.*

### Pending Q&A

Questions awaiting a reply. We expect a brand reply within 5 business days. After that the question is flagged red on your dashboard.

If you don't have an answer (yet), it is acceptable to reply *"We're checking with the maker — we'll update once we hear back."* That counts as a reply and lifts the flag.

### Search Q&A

Same shape as Search Reviews. Full-text plus filters.

### Q&A Options

The configuration panel:

| Setting | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Allow questions on this brand | On | Off pauses incoming questions |
| Default reply signature | Brand name | Or a person's name from your team |
| Auto-acknowledge | Off | If on, sends a "we got your question" auto-message |
| Photo allowed in question | Off | Off prevents customer photos in questions |
| Photo allowed in answer | On | You can include images in your answers |

## Review Options

A larger configuration panel at *Catalog → Reviews → Options*.

| Setting | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Star rating dimensions | Quality, Fit, Color, As-described | Platform-level; not editable. Some dimensions hide where they don't apply (e.g., Fit hides on home goods) |
| Review request email | On | Sent 14 days after delivery |
| Re-request | Never | If you want, set to send once at 28 days if no review |
| Photo uploads | On | Customers can attach photos |
| Verified-buyer badge | Always | Platform-level; cannot disable |
| Review minimum length | None | Platform-level decision |
| Review removal request | Per-policy | You can request removal; platform decides per policy |

## Review Requests

The cadence and content of the review request email is platform-managed. We do not let brands customize the email. Two reasons: (1) consistent customer experience across the marketplace, (2) a brand-customized email risks looking like a marketing email and gets ignored.

You can opt out of review requests for specific products (e.g., a fragile artisan piece where photo reviews could be misleading). Opt-outs are rare; most brands leave the default on.

## Custom Product Forms

A small but useful surface: at *Catalog → Custom Forms*, brands can attach an additional form to specific products. Common uses:

- A *Personalization* form on a piece that can be engraved.
- A *Custom commission* form on a made-to-order piece.
- A *Care preference* form on a piece with multiple finishing options.

Custom forms are reviewed by editorial for clarity and required-field appropriateness before going live. They cannot include payment fields (platform-level; payments go through standard checkout).

## Volume considerations

Brands with 5+ years on the platform sometimes accumulate thousands of reviews. The console handles this fine; the search is fast. For very large review sets, the export-and-analyze workflow is more useful than scrolling.

## Tone reminders

A few practices we have observed work well, drawn from brand operators we admire:

- **Reply to the most thoughtful negative reviews first.** A considered reply to "I love the bag but the strap is too short for me" is more useful than a reply to "5 stars love it!"
- **Don't dismiss critical reviews.** Acknowledge what's true. Customers can tell the difference between defensiveness and honesty.
- **Use review patterns to inform listings.** Recurring fit comments → update the sizing translation. Recurring care confusion → improve the care text. The data is there; act on it.

A reminder we extend in good faith: you cannot delete reviews for being negative. We do not honor those requests. But we will work with you on legitimate removal grounds (defamatory content, off-topic, mistaken submission) — see the platform-level policy doc.
