## How shipping works at SmartCartCommerce

Most items ship from the maker's own studio. A subset — primarily inventory we hold on consignment for high-velocity brands — ships from one of four bonded fulfillment centers (New Jersey, Los Angeles, London, Hong Kong). Both paths are managed end-to-end by our logistics team. The customer experience is the same.

We support shipping to 64 countries. We do not currently ship to: Russia, Belarus, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Venezuela (sanctions); Nigeria, Ghana, Pakistan (carrier reliability — under review).

## Standard delivery windows

Windows below are calendar days from order placement, including any required production time for made-to-order items. They include both handling and transit.

| Region | Standard | Express | Same-day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Continental US | 4–7 days | 1–2 days | NYC, LA, SF, Chicago |
| Canada | 5–9 days | 2–3 days | Toronto, Vancouver |
| UK & Ireland | 3–6 days | 1–2 days | London Zones 1–3 |
| EU (Schengen) | 3–7 days | 1–3 days | Paris, Milan, Berlin |
| Switzerland & UK (post-Brexit) | 5–9 days | 2–4 days | — |
| Japan | 5–10 days | 2–4 days | Tokyo 23-ku |

Made-to-order pieces (most jewelry, some furniture, all bespoke fashion) add the maker's stated production lead time on top of the shipping window. The product page always shows an estimated arrival range that includes both.

## Shipping costs

Shipping is free, on standard, on orders over the following thresholds: $150 USD (US), £120 (UK), €140 (EU), ¥18,000 (JP), AU$220 (AU/NZ).

Below the threshold, standard shipping is between $9 and $24 USD equivalent depending on origin and destination. Express is always paid (varies by destination). Same-day, where offered, is a flat $30 USD equivalent.

SmartCart Circle members ship free on standard, in all markets, regardless of order value.

## What we do that other shippers don't

**Consolidation.** If you place two orders within 48 hours that ship from the same origin, we will hold the first to ship them together. We will email you to ask first.

**Re-route mid-shipment.** Up until the package is on the final-mile vehicle, you can change the delivery address from your account. This is unusual; most carriers don't support it. We invested in carrier integrations that do.

**No-contact delivery is signed by us.** We use a photographic-handoff signature where required, but we never leave a package in the open. If you are not home and we cannot locate a safe placement, we hold and re-attempt. We do not push the burden onto the carrier.

**Customs handled.** For international orders, we calculate duties and taxes at checkout and remit them on your behalf via a Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) arrangement. You will not get a surprise customs bill at the door.

## Delays

If your order is going to miss its estimated window by more than 48 hours, we email proactively. We do not wait for you to ask. The email includes the new estimate, the cause, and (for Circle members) a $20 credit toward your next order, applied automatically.

## Insurance

Every order is fully insured against loss in transit at no cost to you. If an order is lost, we replace or refund — your choice — within one business day of confirming the loss with the carrier.

## Carrier partners

We work with UPS, FedEx, USPS, DHL Express, Royal Mail, La Poste, Yamato, Australia Post, and a regional partner network of approximately 40 last-mile carriers. The first three (UPS, FedEx, USPS) are our primary US-side carriers; the rest provide international coverage. We choose carriers based on destination and item, not on cost alone. A heavy ceramic vase will not travel by the cheapest carrier.

## Pickup options

In New York, Los Angeles, London, and Tokyo, we offer in-showroom pickup at no charge. Orders placed before 11am local time can usually be picked up by 5pm the same day, subject to maker location.
