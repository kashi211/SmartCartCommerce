# Order Cancellation Policy

**Document type:** Customer-facing policy
**Owner:** Saket Panwar
**Last reviewed:** 06 February 2026

---

## The short version

You can cancel any order before it ships. Most orders ship within 24–72 hours of placement, so the window is short — but real. Once a package is in the carrier's hands, the order can no longer be cancelled, and you would instead use the return process.

For made-to-order, made-to-measure, and pre-order items, the cancellation window is longer (because production hasn't begun), but it closes the moment the maker starts work.

## How to cancel

The fastest way is your account: go to the order, click *Cancel order*. The button disappears the moment the package is in carrier custody.

You can also reply to the order confirmation email with the word "cancel," or message your concierge if you are a SmartCart Circle member. We treat all three channels with the same priority.

## Cancellation timing rules

**Stocked items (ships from fulfillment center).** Cancel any time before the carrier scan. After the scan, return process applies.

**Stocked items (ships direct from maker).** Same rule, but the carrier scan tends to happen 24–48 hours later, so the window is generally a little longer.

**Made-to-order.** You have a 24-hour grace window after placing the order during which cancellation is unconditional. After that, cancellation is permitted up until the maker logs the start of production in our system. Once started, the order can no longer be cancelled, but it can be modified by mutual agreement (size, finish, configuration) at the maker's discretion.

**Made-to-measure / bespoke.** No cancellation after the second consultation conversation. The brief, by that point, is committed work for the maker.

**Pre-orders.** Free cancellation any time before stock arrival.

**Subscription / Curated Boxes.** Cancel any time, no fees. If you cancel within 7 days of a billing date, that month's box still ships and the cancellation applies to the next cycle.

**Gift cards.** Non-cancellable, but transferrable.

## Partial cancellation

You can cancel one or more items in a multi-item order without cancelling the rest. Use the same flow; the system will allow per-line-item cancellation.

If a partial cancellation drops your order below a free-shipping threshold, we will not retroactively charge you the shipping difference. We absorb it.

## What happens after you cancel

The hold on your card is released. Most banks reflect the release within 1–3 business days. If you paid by gift card or store credit, the credit is restored to your account within minutes.

If we cancel an order on our end — typically because of a stockout or a maker pull — we email you within an hour, refund within the same hour, and apply a $25 credit to your next order. We treat a maker-side cancellation as a service failure on our part, even when the cause is upstream.

## Disputes about cancellation timing

We log every cancellation action with a timestamp. If there is ever a dispute about whether an order was cancelled in time, we share the log with you and rule in your favor on close calls. We do not chase a $40 candle into a $400 customer relationship.
