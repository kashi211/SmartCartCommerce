## Why we ship internationally well

International e-commerce is full of small frustrations: surprise customs bills, two-week delays at borders, packages stuck in clearance with no explanation, items returned because the recipient didn't realize duties were unpaid. We have invested heavily in not doing those things.

This document explains what we do, market by market.

## Duties and taxes — Delivered Duty Paid (DDP)

For all international orders, we calculate import duties and taxes at checkout and remit them on your behalf. The price you see at checkout is the total price you will pay. There is no second bill at the door. There is no signature-required customs payment.

We use a partner (Zonos for the Americas; Hurricane for EMEA + APAC) to compute landed cost in real time. The numbers are accurate within ~1.5% of carrier-assessed duty. If the carrier assesses a higher duty than we estimated, we cover the difference.

Some specific country notes:

**United Kingdom.** All orders ship DDP, including post-Brexit VAT calculation. The VAT is collected by us and remitted via our UK VAT registration. Items declared at the carrier as "duty paid" do not generate a UK courier collection charge.

**European Union.** All EU orders ship DDP including IOSS-applicable VAT for orders ≤€150 and standard import VAT/duty for orders >€150.

**Switzerland.** DDP. The 7.7% Swiss VAT (8.1% as of January 2026) is included at checkout.

**Norway.** DDP. We are registered under the Norwegian VOEC scheme.

**United States.** Orders shipped to US addresses from non-US origins arrive with all duties paid. Section 321 de minimis does not apply to most of our orders (we err on the side of declaring full value); duties are paid by us regardless.

**Canada.** DDP including GST/HST/PST as applicable. We are registered for GST/HST under the simplified regime.

**Japan.** DDP including JCT (10%). We are registered as a Qualified Invoice Issuer.

**Australia.** DDP including 10% GST. Registered as an electronic distribution platform under the simplified regime.

## What we cannot help with

A handful of countries impose import licenses or restrictions on specific categories that we cannot pre-clear. The most common cases:

**Cosmetics into China and Korea** — many of our beauty brands cannot be imported into mainland China without specific brand-side registration. Where this is the case, the product page will be unavailable in those markets at checkout.

**Food and alcohol** — heavily restricted across most markets. We ship our food category to a subset of about 18 markets and do not ship alcohol to Saudi Arabia, UAE (alcohol only), Brunei, or markets where alcohol shipment by mail is prohibited.

**Plant-based items in Australia and New Zealand** — strict biosecurity rules apply. Some natural-fiber home goods and certain food items cannot pass customs. Where applicable, we either delist for those markets or pre-treat in line with biosecurity requirements.

We do not use ocean freight for any retail shipments.

## Returns from outside our home markets

International returns are handled through prepaid labels we provide. The returner does not deal with customs documentation; we handle re-import as a returned-goods entry. Any duties we paid on the original outbound shipment are reclaimed where possible and refunded to you regardless.

## Tracking quality

A common frustration internationally: a package with no scans for ten days. We invest in carrier integrations that surface micro-events (transit handover, customs clearance, line-haul scan). If your tracker has been silent for more than 72 hours, your concierge can pull a private status from the carrier within minutes. Do not wait — message us.
