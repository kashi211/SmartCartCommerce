# Sizing & Fit Guide

## Why sizing is hard at SmartCartCommerce

Independent makers do not use the same size charts. A small-batch designer in Tokyo may grade by a Japanese 9–13 system. A boutique workshop in Florence may grade by Italian 38–48 with their own internal "house fit." A custom shirtmaker may publish only neck and sleeve length and assume the customer knows their measurements. Adding to this, our brands often work in deadstock fabric, which means a single garment may be cut from a textile that runs slightly differently than the next batch.

We do not believe in pretending all of our brands fit the same way. Instead, we make fit information specific, measurable, and easy to compare. Every product page does the work for you.

## What every product page shows

For wearable items, every product page includes:

**Brand size system** — the size system the maker uses (e.g., IT, JP, US, UK, EU).

**SmartCartCommerce translation** — our flat-measurement translation into a single common reference. Every size is a precise garment measurement (chest, waist, hip, sleeve, hem) in centimeters and inches.

**Model reference** — the height, size in this garment, and any styling note (e.g., "size up if between" or "true to brand size").

**Fit personality** — one of *trim*, *clean*, *easy*, *generous*, or *oversized*. This is a curatorial reading from our editorial team, not a maker claim.

**Fabric behavior** — how the textile drapes, stretches, settles after a wash, or relaxes with wear. We add this where it matters.

We do not use vanity sizing. If a piece runs small, the product page says so.

## The SmartCartCommerce measure-yourself guide

If you do not know your measurements, our website hosts a 4-minute guided self-measurement flow that asks for chest/bust, waist, low waist, hip, inseam, sleeve, and shoulder. We store these in your profile so you do not need to repeat them.

Once stored, every product page shows a **Fits you / Likely fits / Likely doesn't fit** indicator. This is not a guarantee. It is a probabilistic read against the garment's flat measurements with a tolerance built in.

## Footwear

Footwear sizing is even messier than apparel. We publish foot length in centimeters (heel to longest toe), foot width category (narrow / standard / wide), and any toe-shape notes (round, square, almond). The maker's regional size is shown alongside.

Most footwear brands on SmartCartCommerce offer free first-fit exchange — you can swap a size once with no return shipping cost.

## Jewelry

Rings: sized using the SmartCartCommerce ring sizer (mailable or printable; both work). Most fine jewelry brands on the platform offer free first-time resizing within 60 days. Bangles and bracelets are measured in inner circumference.

Necklaces: chain length is published in cm and inches, with a position note ("collarbone," "between collarbone and bust," "below bust").

## Accessories

Hats: head circumference at the widest part, in cm. Belts: length from buckle pin to middle hole, in cm. Glasses: lens width / bridge / temple length, in mm.

## Fit support from concierge

If you are between sizes, concierge can help. They have access to a database of all our brands' fit personalities, returns history by SKU, and known quirks. A typical concierge fit conversation takes 4–6 minutes.

For Circle members, your concierge can also pull the model's measurements (where the model has consented to share them) and other published references for a more detailed comparison.

## What if it doesn't fit

Free returns and free exchanges within the 60-day window. We strongly prefer exchange to refund for sizing — it keeps you in the piece you wanted. For made-to-measure or made-to-order pieces, see the relevant policy.
