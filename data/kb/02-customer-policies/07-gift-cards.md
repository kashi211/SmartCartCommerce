# Gift Cards

**Document type:** Customer-facing policy
**Owner:** Saket Panwar
**Last reviewed:** 04 January 2026

## What we offer

SmartCartCommerce gift cards are available in two formats: a digital code delivered by email, and a physical card pressed in cotton paper, hand-numbered, and mailed in a small linen envelope. Both are funded in any amount between $25 and $5,000 USD equivalent.

## How they work

The recipient receives a code (or scans the QR on the back of the physical card) and applies it at checkout. The balance is deducted as items are purchased. Unused balance remains on the card.

A gift card is effectively store credit linked to a code. We do not require the recipient to create an account to use one, though doing so makes balance tracking easier.

## Validity

Gift cards are valid for **10 years** from the issue date. We chose 10 years because it is the longest period permissible across all markets we operate in, and because we believe a gift should feel like a gift, not a deadline. Some marketplaces let cards expire after 12 or 18 months. We find that practice contemptuous.

If you have a card that has technically expired (more than 10 years old), contact concierge. We will reissue it at full original value.

## What you cannot do

You cannot redeem a gift card for cash, except where required by law (some US states require a small remaining-balance redemption — we comply automatically).

You cannot resell a gift card. Codes that appear on resale platforms are voided. We monitor.

You cannot apply a gift card to a SmartCart Circle membership purchase. Circle is a paid relationship and we want it to be funded by the member, not gifted on a one-off. (Memberships can, however, be purchased *as* a gift; that is a separate flow.)

## Lost or stolen cards

If a digital card was sent to the wrong email address, contact concierge. We can reissue if the original code has not been redeemed.

If a physical card is lost: if you have the card number, we can reissue. If you do not, we are unable to recover it. We treat physical cards like cash for this reason. Most customers prefer the digital format.

## Refunds and gift cards

If you purchase a gift card and want a refund, we issue one within the 60-day return window, provided the card has not been redeemed. After 60 days, the purchase is non-refundable, but the card remains valid for its full 10-year term.

If a recipient returns an item paid for with a gift card, the refund returns to the gift card balance, not to your original purchasing card.

## Bulk and corporate gifting

Companies frequently buy SmartCartCommerce cards as employee gifts or client gifts. For orders over $5,000 in aggregate, we offer concierge-coordinated bulk gifting: custom packaging, hand-written notes, batch delivery, and (for orders above $25,000) a dedicated account manager for the season.

Email *gifting@smartcartcommerce.com* for bulk inquiries. Lead time for bulk gifting in November/December is 21 days minimum; we close the holiday gifting window on 10 December.

## Fraud and abuse

We see attempted gift-card fraud — typically phishing-driven attempts to acquire codes. If anyone calls, emails, or messages you claiming to be SmartCartCommerce and asks you to read the code from a gift card, it is fraud. We will never do that. Forward suspicious messages to *security@smartcartcommerce.com*.
