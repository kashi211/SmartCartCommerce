## What this covers

This policy applies when something arrives damaged, breaks during normal use within the warranty period, or fails to function as described. It is separate from the standard 60-day return process — this exists because the right answer is not "send it back for a refund."

## If your item arrives damaged

Take a photo of the package and a photo of the item before doing anything else. Email *concierge@smartcartcommerce.com* with the order number and photos within 14 days of delivery. We do not require unboxing video.

We then choose between three responses based on the item:

**Replace.** Default for stocked goods. We dispatch a replacement within 24 hours of confirming the damage. Damaged item: keep, donate, or send back in the same prepaid pouch — your choice.

**Repair.** Default for furniture, ceramics with chips, jewelry, watches, and certain leather goods. Our partner network of restorers is generally able to repair within 14–28 days. We arrange pickup; we cover all costs.

**Refund.** Default if neither replacement nor repair is feasible (one-of-a-kind item, end-of-edition stock, allergic reaction to a beauty product). Issued within one business day of confirmation.

Customer chooses if more than one is feasible.

## If your item breaks in normal use

Every item on SmartCartCommerce is covered by what we call our **Care Promise**, which works as follows:

| Category | Coverage period | What's covered |
|---|---|---|
| Fashion | 12 months | Manufacturing defects, seam failure, hardware failure |
| Home & Living (textiles) | 24 months | Manufacturing defects, dye stability, structural integrity |
| Home & Living (furniture) | 5 years | Frame and joinery |
| Beauty | At purchase | Defective formulation, contamination, broken seal |
| Food & Beverage | Through expiration | Spoilage prior to expiration date when stored as directed |
| Jewelry (fine) | Lifetime | Maker defects, structural integrity, stone setting |
| Watches | 5 years | Movement defects, water resistance to stated rating |

Coverage applies to the original purchaser. Wear-and-tear is not covered, but our paid repair service is available throughout the life of the item, and SmartCart Circle members have a $200 annual repair credit.

## If your item is not as described

Contact concierge. We define this broadly: wrong color, wrong size, wrong material, wrong configuration, wrong scent profile (where the listing made specific claims). We default to the customer's interpretation of "as described" unless the description on the page was unambiguously precise about the variation. We will not argue you out of a return because of a turn of phrase.

## What we do behind the scenes

When we receive a damage report, we open a case and tag it to the maker. The maker sees the report (anonymized). If a brand accumulates more than 1.5% damage rate over a rolling 90-day window, our quality team initiates a review with the brand to identify and fix the cause. This is not a "deduct from their payouts" exercise; it is a partnership investigation. We pay for the customer remedy ourselves and recover from the brand only if there is clear maker fault under our seller agreement.

## What we won't do

We will not ask you to ship a fragile broken item back at your cost. We will not require photographs of the damaged item placed next to ID. We will not quote our policy at you in defense; we will quote our policy at ourselves to ensure we do the right thing.
