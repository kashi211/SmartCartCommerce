By using SmartCartCommerce, you agree to (1) be honest with us about who you are, (2) pay for what you order, (3) not abuse the platform — meaning no fraud, no resale-for-laundering, no scraping, no brigading our reviews. Either side can stop using the service at any time. If we have a serious disagreement, we have a process below.

That is the whole shape of it.

## 1. Who can use SmartCartCommerce

You must be 18 or older. You must provide accurate information at registration. One person can hold one account; one organization can hold one account. We do not allow account-sharing for our subscription products.

## 2. Orders, prices, and acceptance

When you place an order, you are making an offer to buy at the listed price. We accept your offer when we email you the shipping confirmation, not at order placement. If we discover a pricing error before that point — for instance, a decimal-point typo on a watch — we will contact you to confirm the correct price or cancel the order with full refund. We have used this clause four times in five years and we will continue to use it sparingly.

## 3. Payment

We accept the major card networks, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and a small set of regional methods (iDEAL, Bancontact, Konbini in Japan). We charge at order placement, not at shipment. For made-to-order items above $1,500 USD, we may take a 30% deposit at order and the balance at production completion; this is shown clearly at checkout.

## 4. Right of withdrawal (EU/UK)

In addition to our 60-day return policy (which is more generous than required), EU and UK customers have a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal under the Consumer Rights Directive / UK Consumer Contracts Regulations. Excluded categories follow the directive: bespoke goods, perishable goods, sealed goods unsealed for hygiene reasons, and so on.
