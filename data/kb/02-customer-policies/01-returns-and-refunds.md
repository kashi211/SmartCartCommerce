# Returns & Refunds Policy

**Document type:** Customer-facing policy
**Owner:** Saket Panwar
**Last reviewed:** 18 March 2026
**Effective:** 01 April 2026 onward.

---

## In one paragraph

You have 60 days to return most items for a full refund. Some categories — perishable food, personalized jewelry, opened beauty — are exceptions, and we list them below. Return shipping is free in 14 markets and discounted in another 22. Refunds land on your original payment method, usually within 5 business days of us receiving the item, and we will tell you what's happening at every step.

## The 60-day window

The clock starts the day your order is delivered. You can request a return up to 60 days from that date. We do not require a reason and we do not penalize repeat returners.

If you have an issue past 60 days — a defect, a craftsmanship failure, a piece of furniture that has wobbled into existence — please contact concierge anyway. We have a separate after-care path for those cases (see *Damaged or Defective Items*).

## Categories with exceptions

Most exceptions exist for hygiene, regulation, or because the item was made for you specifically.

**Beauty & Wellness — opened items.** Sealed and unopened: full 60-day window. Opened: returnable within 14 days only if there is a sensitivity reaction or formulation concern. We do not restock opened beauty; returned opened items are donated to product-development partners or destroyed.

**Artisan Food & Beverage — perishables.** Non-returnable once dispatched, except in the case of damage, spoilage on arrival, or allergen mislabeling. We err on the side of replacement or refund without requiring a return.

**Fine Jewelry & Watches — personalized.** Engraved, sized, or commissioned pieces are non-returnable. Stock pieces follow the standard 60-day window. Servicing and resizing are covered separately under *Jewelry Care & Authentication*.

**Fashion — final-sale items.** A small number of items are marked final sale on the product page (typically end-of-edition or vintage). We do not accept returns on these. The badge is shown on the product page, in the cart, and at checkout. We will not surprise you with it.

**Home & Living — bespoke or made-to-order.** A piece commissioned to your specifications — a sofa in your fabric choice, a table at a custom length — is non-returnable. Stock home goods follow the standard window.

## Condition

Returned items must be in original condition with original packaging where possible. We are not strict about minor things — a tag re-tied, a folded shirt re-folded, a candle that has been smelled. We are strict about wear and damage. If an item comes back visibly used, our team will document it and a partial refund may apply.

## How to start a return

Three options. (1) Visit your account, choose the order, click *Start a return*. (2) Reply to the order confirmation email with the word "return" and the items. (3) Message your concierge if you are a SmartCart Circle member.

You will receive a return label by email within 30 minutes during business hours, or first thing the next morning otherwise.

## Free return shipping markets

Continental US, Canada, UK, Ireland, France, Spain, Portugal, Germany, Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg, Italy, Sweden, Denmark.

In all other markets, return shipping is at a discounted flat rate (we cover the difference between commercial and our negotiated rate). Customers in Australia, Japan, Singapore, Hong Kong, and the UAE pay between $9 and $24 USD equivalent.

## Refund timing

We refund within 1 business day of the returned item arriving at the receiving fulfillment center and being scanned in. Funds typically appear on your original payment method within 3–5 business days after that, depending on your bank.

If you paid by gift card or store credit, refunds return to the same instrument within minutes.

## What you will not get

You will not get a notification asking you to fill out a satisfaction survey on a return. You will not get an email asking why you returned. You will not be offered a coupon to keep the item. We do not believe in those tactics. If a return is the right outcome, we want it to be a frictionless one.
