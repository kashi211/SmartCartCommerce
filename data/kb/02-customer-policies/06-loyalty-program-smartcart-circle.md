# SmartCart Circle — Membership Program

**Document type:** Customer-facing program description
**Owner:** Saket Panwar
**Last reviewed:** 09 March 2026

---

## What it is

SmartCart Circle is our paid annual membership. It costs $240 USD per year (or local equivalent: £190, €220, ¥34,000, AU$360). It is the single most useful relationship a frequent customer can have with us.

There is no points system. There are no tiers. There is no spend-to-unlock structure. It is one membership, one fee, one set of benefits.

## What you get

**A named concierge.** When you join, you are introduced to a member of our concierge team who becomes your point of contact. Real human, named, with a calendar. Reachable by message, email, phone, or in-person at one of our showrooms. Same person every time.

**Free standard shipping, all markets, no minimum.** No threshold. A $40 candle ships free. A $4,000 watch ships free.

**Free returns, all markets.** Same.

**Early access to new releases.** Most maker drops are visible to Circle members 48 hours before public listing. About 12% of new releases sell out within that window.

**Members-only items.** A small number of items per quarter — typically commissioned by us with brands — are available only to Circle members. Recent examples: a 25-piece run of hand-finished silver tea spoons by a Kyoto silversmith; a single-edition cashmere blanket from an Edinburgh weaver.

**Birthday gift.** A piece chosen by your concierge each year, in the $50–$80 range, hand-wrapped, no charge.

**Showroom access.** Private appointment booking at our New York, Los Angeles, London, and Tokyo showrooms. Including after-hours viewings on request.

**Repair credit.** $200 USD per year toward repair, alteration, or restoration via our partner network. Unused credit does not roll over.

**Take-back priority.** Take-back collection scheduled within 7 days, vs. 21–30 days for non-members.

**A printed quarterly journal.** Our editorial team produces a small print magazine, mailed quarterly. It is a real magazine — about 80 pages — with brand visits, editorial features, and a recipe section. Members are the only people who get it.

## What you don't get

You do not get a points balance. You do not get gamified streaks. You do not get *exclusive* sale access (we do not run sales). You do not get tier upgrades. You do not get the membership free-of-charge in exchange for a marketing email signup. You pay for it. That is the design.

## Cancellation and refund

Cancel any time, in your account or via concierge. If you cancel within 14 days of signing up and have not used material benefits, we refund in full. After 14 days, we refund a pro-rata portion based on months remaining in the annual cycle. Used birthday gifts and shipped journals are not deducted.

## Eligibility

Anyone with a SmartCartCommerce account in a country we ship to. Single-person membership; not transferable. We do not currently offer family or household memberships, but we are exploring it.

## Why we run it this way

We could run a free, points-based loyalty program. Most of our peers do. We have chosen not to because we do not want our customer to feel they are inside a system designed to extract more spending. We want them to feel they are inside a relationship.

The membership pays for the concierge service. It pays for the journal. It pays for the early-access logistics. We run it close to break-even on purpose. The membership is not a profit center; it is the budget for the level of attention we want every Circle member to receive.

## Membership numbers

At the time of writing, SmartCart Circle has approximately 41,000 members. Annual renewal rate is 86%. Average member spends 4.3x what a non-member spends, but we treat that statistic with caution — selection bias is real.
