# Privacy Policy

**Document type:** Legal / customer-facing
**Owner:** Saket Panwar
**Last reviewed:** 03 March 2026
**Plain-language summary first; full legal terms follow.**

---

## In plain language

We collect the minimum information we need to sell you something, ship it to you, and provide service if it goes wrong. We do not sell your data. We do not run third-party advertising on our site, and we do not run advertising-driven surveillance. We do not share your data with our brands except for what's strictly necessary to fulfill an order. You can ask us at any time to show you what we have, correct it, export it, or delete it.

There. That is the whole spirit of this document.

## What we collect

**You give us:** name, shipping and billing addresses, email, phone (optional), payment method (tokenized via our payment partners — we never store full card numbers), order history, sizing and preference information you choose to provide, communications with our team.

**We observe:** which pages you visit on our site, which products you save, what you click in our emails, which device you visit from. We use this for operational analytics (improving the site) and for the personal-stylist feature (only if you opt in).

**We do not collect:** your activity on other websites. We do not run ad-tracking pixels for third parties on our pages.

## Cookies

We use cookies for three things only: keeping you signed in, remembering your cart, and our own first-party analytics. There is no consent banner because we do not run third-party tracking. Our analytics is privacy-respecting (we use a self-hosted tool that does not generate cross-site identifiers).

## Who sees your data

Internal: only the smallest set of teammates whose work requires it. Concierge sees order history. Logistics sees shipping address. Finance sees payment status. Engineers do not see customer data in their normal work; access for debugging is logged and time-limited.

External: our payment processors (Stripe, Adyen) see what they need to charge you. Carriers see what they need to ship to you. Tax authorities see what they are entitled to see in the relevant jurisdiction. Brands see your name, shipping address, and order details for their items only — and only after the order is placed.

We do not sell your data. We do not "share for advertising purposes" within the meaning of US state privacy laws.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you have the right to (a) know what we have about you, (b) correct it if it's wrong, (c) get a copy in a portable format, (d) ask us to delete it, and (e) limit how we use it.

To exercise any of these, email *privacy@smartcartcommerce.com* or use the *Privacy* page in your account. We respond within 30 days, often within 3.

In the EU, EEA, UK, and Switzerland, your legal rights under GDPR or local equivalent are not diminished by anything in this policy.

In California, your CCPA/CPRA rights are not diminished by anything in this policy. To exercise the *Do Not Sell or Share* right, even though we don't sell or share for advertising, use the form on our website. We will confirm your status.

## How long we keep things

Order records: 7 years (for tax). Communications: 3 years. Browse history: 13 months. Account information: until you delete the account, or 5 years after last use.

If you delete your account, we delete what we are not legally required to retain. We do not maintain "shadow" copies for marketing purposes.

## Children

SmartCartCommerce is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If we learn we have, we delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in ways that materially affect customers, we email registered customers at least 30 days in advance. We do not bury changes in fine print.
